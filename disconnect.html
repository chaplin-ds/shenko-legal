Disconnecting from QuickBooks

Access can be revoked at any time. Disconnecting immediately ends the integration's ability to read that company file.

From QuickBooks Online:

  1. Sign in as an administrator of the company.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon) → Apps → My Apps.
  3. Find Shenko Group Accounting Integration and choose Disconnect.

Once disconnected, stored authorisation tokens for that company are no longer valid and are removed from the integration host.

To request disconnection or ask about data held: danny@shenkogroup.com
