Connecting to QuickBooks

The Shenko Group Accounting Integration is private internal software. It connects only to QuickBooks Online company files owned by Shenko Group, and connections are made by the business owner.

To connect or reconnect a company file:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator of that company.
  2. Run the authorisation process from the integration host and approve access when Intuit prompts.
  3. The connection is confirmed once the company appears in the integration's connected list.

If a connection has expired, repeating the same steps restores it in place.
