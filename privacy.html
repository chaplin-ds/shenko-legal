Privacy Policy

Shenko Group Accounting Integration

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What this application is

The Shenko Group Accounting Integration is private, internal software operated by Shenko Group for its own businesses. It is not offered to the public, not listed in any app marketplace, and has no users other than the business owner and staff he authorises.

What data it accesses

The application connects to QuickBooks Online company files owned and controlled by Shenko Group. Through Intuit's API it reads accounting records belonging to those companies, including:

  • Chart of accounts, journal entries and transactions
  • Invoices, payments, deposits and expenses
  • Vendor and customer records attached to those transactions
  • Financial reports such as profit and loss and balance sheets

It accesses only company files that Shenko Group owns, and only after an authorised administrator of each company has explicitly granted access through Intuit's standard consent flow.

Why it accesses that data

Financial reporting, bookkeeping review, reconciliation and month-end close across the group's locations. The data is used solely to run and understand the group's own businesses.

Who can see the data

The business owner, and employees or professional advisers he authorises — a bookkeeper or accountant acting for the business. No other person or organisation has access.

Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Processing takes place on a single private computer owned by the business. There is no cloud hosting and no third-party processor.
  • The host machine uses full-disk encryption (BitLocker, XTS-AES-256).
  • Communication with Intuit uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Internal access is restricted to an encrypted private network with no internet-facing ports, and requires token authentication.
  • Administrative rights are separated from the account that runs the integration.

Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared or disclosed to any third party. There is no advertising, no analytics provider and no data broker involved at any point.

Data retention

Accounting data is read on demand for reporting rather than warehoused. Derived reports are retained for as long as the business needs them for its own records. OAuth tokens are stored locally and are revoked when a connection is removed.

Your choices

An administrator of a connected QuickBooks company can disconnect the application at any time from within QuickBooks Online, or by contacting us. Disconnection immediately revokes the application's access to that company file.
